# Semantic Authority

## Root semantic authority

The canonical meaning of human function remains:

> Human function is the body's capacity to be appropriately engaged to meet internal and external demands.

The current authoritative UOHF publication governs UOHF object definitions.

## HFWM authority

HFWM documents may organize UOHF objects into a world-model architecture but may not silently redefine:

- human function;
- Capacity;
- Functional Engagement;
- Demand;
- evidence;
- UOHF concept identities;
- UOHF formal relations.

If an HFWM research result requires a change to UOHF itself, that change must enter UOHF governance separately.

## Functional Bridge authority

Functional Bridge is explanatory knowledge. It is not a shortcut for changing UOHF or establishing individual facts.
