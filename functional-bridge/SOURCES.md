# Public Reference Sources for Functional Bridge

Inclusion here does not mean automatic formalization into UOHF and does not make general knowledge person-specific evidence.

## Human Reference Atlas (HRA)

Börner K, Teichmann SA, Quardokus EM, et al. **Anatomical structures, cell types and biomarkers of the Human Reference Atlas.** *Nature Cell Biology.* 2021;23:1117–1128.  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-021-00788-6

**HFWM role:** anatomical, cellular, biomarker and spatial reference knowledge.
