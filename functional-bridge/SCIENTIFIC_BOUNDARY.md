# Functional Bridge Scientific Boundary

The following distinctions are permanent unless explicitly revised by formal governance:

```text
Ontology ≠ Knowledge
Knowledge ≠ Evidence
Functional Bridge ≠ Evidence
Reference standard ≠ Individual fact
General relation ≠ Individual causal attribution
Record ≠ General scientific knowledge
```

Functional Bridge can support explanation, retrieval, hypothesis generation and scientific traceability.

It cannot by itself establish:

- individual diagnosis;
- individual cause;
- current capacity deficit;
- current engagement problem;
- safety clearance;
- treatment prescription;
- professional authority.

Any transition from general knowledge to person-specific judgment requires admissible individual evidence and the applicable governance/authority rules.
