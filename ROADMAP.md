# HFWM Public Research Roadmap

The public research roadmap is intentionally separated from proprietary product implementation.

## Research track A — formal world-state model
Typed person state, time, context, history, task, Demand, capacity, engagement, response, evidence, action and update.

## Research track B — internal tasks
Defense, hemostasis, regulation, repair, recovery, adaptation and other governed internal-task families.

## Research track C — capacity coordinate
Validation and evolution of the public 18-core/104-specific capacity system.

## Research track D — Functional Bridge
Public scientific propositions linking human function to structure, physiology, multiscale mechanism, response and change.

## Research track E — professional/life coordination
Responsibility routing across medicine, nursing, PT, OT, SLP, audiology, dietetics, psychology, exercise/training, manual practice, caregivers and environment.

## Research track F — longitudinal update
History-preserving state change, reassessment, report projection and prospective validation.

## Research track G — empirical world-model benchmarks
Prediction, calibration, uncertainty, routing, safety escalation, historical consistency and falsification.

No roadmap item implies a commitment to open-source proprietary production implementation.
