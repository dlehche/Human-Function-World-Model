# Whole-Person Scope

HFWM uses **the same whole person** as the persistent modeled entity.

Whole-person modeling means continuity of identity across:

- molecular/cellular/organ knowledge when relevant;
- physiology and regulation;
- disease, injury and medical treatment;
- human-function capacity and actual engagement;
- cognition, emotion and behavior;
- self-care, mobility, communication, work, exercise and participation;
- professional services;
- family, caregiving and environment;
- time and longitudinal history.

HFWM does not claim exhaustive representation of every possible variable. Explicit unknowns are valid model states.
