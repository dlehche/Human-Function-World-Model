# Human-Readable World-State Projection

A Body Function Report is not the world model itself.

A public abstraction is:

```text
Report_t = Projection(ModelState_t, Evidence_t, Audience, Authority)
```

A person-facing report may prioritize:

- current state;
- major functional manifestations;
- change over time;
- current boundary;
- next step;
- follow-up conditions.

A professional-facing projection may additionally include:

- professional judgment;
- evidence basis;
- uncertainty;
- risk boundary;
- unresolved information;
- professional recommendation;
- reassessment conditions.

Historical projections must remain replayable under the evidence and rules available at the time. Later evidence must not silently rewrite a historical report.
