# Evidence, Uncertainty, and Judgment

HFWM separates reality, evidence, inference and action.

## Evidence can include

- person report;
- professional observation;
- measurements and tests;
- device data;
- medical records;
- formally accepted prior results;
- execution and recovery feedback.

## Evidence is purpose-relative

Evidence sufficient to justify observation may be insufficient to justify diagnosis, intervention, formal state attribution or long-term prediction.

## Unknown is a legitimate state

HFWM must be able to represent:

- not assessed;
- insufficient evidence;
- conflicting evidence;
- competing hypotheses;
- undetermined state.

The model must not convert lack of evidence into a negative fact and must not convert a general scientific relation into an individual causal conclusion.
