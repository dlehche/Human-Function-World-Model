# Governed Action and Longitudinal Update

HFWM action is broader than treatment.

A governed next action may include:

- answer directly;
- observe;
- wait;
- collect more information;
- assess;
- reassess;
- continue;
- modify;
- regress;
- reduce demand;
- modify environment;
- pause;
- stop;
- medical escalation;
- professional referral or transfer;
- self-management;
- rehabilitation;
- exercise/training;
- maintenance;
- follow-up.

Actions are constrained by evidence, safety, authority, feasibility, professional scope, stop conditions and reassessment needs.

Observed change after action becomes new evidence. The longitudinal model may then support, weaken, contradict or leave unresolved the prior judgment.

Historical facts are preserved; later evidence must not silently rewrite what was known at an earlier time.
