## Research question / 科学问题

When do forecasts or outputs from existing medical, physiological, health and computational models support warranted, revisable answers about the **same whole person** across internal physiological demands, disease, work, cognition, environmental assistance, individual priorities and longitudinal change?

现有医学、人体生理、健康和计算模型的结果，在什么条件下，才能支持对**同一个完整的人**形成有依据、可修订的跨领域判断？

## Scope and contribution / 内容与贡献

- Source-qualified comparison of selected medical, physiological and health world models and established whole-person frameworks, without presenting them as failed systems outside their intended use
- Comparison with digital-twin foundations in probabilistic state estimation, modular model composition and complex-system modeling
- Three conditional arguments concerning answer-relevant distinctions, explanatory non-identifiability and the difference between prediction and legitimate action
- Demand–capacity–actual-engagement relationships as a **proposed, testable functional interpretation specification**, not a new universal physiological law
- Constructed person-level cases, a public assessment-protocol example and an explicit conditional scheduling-and-support derivation
- Matched-information, strong-baseline and falsification-oriented evaluation proposals, including separation of newly supplied information from additional modeling or interpretation

本文以完整的人为持续建模对象，区分已有模型输出的有效用途、证据不足、信息缺口和解释关系缺口；讨论人体功能的需求—能力—实际调用关系，提供构造性检验情景和可反证的比较方案。**概念定义、演算示例和未来研究计划不等于完成了实证效果验证。**
