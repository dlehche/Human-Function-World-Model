# Root hypothesis manuscript

## Human function as a demand-conditioned organising coordinate for whole-person medicine: a testable hypothesis

[English full text](HYPOTHESIS_EN.md) · [中文完整对照稿](HYPOTHESIS_ZH.md) · [HFWM framework paper](../hfwm-framework/HFWM_EN.md)

This manuscript states the **root empirical hypothesis** underlying the current Human Function World Model research program:

> Human function may provide a demand-conditioned organising coordinate for the same person across biological, clinical, behavioural, environmental, and temporal contexts.

The manuscript deliberately distinguishes the root hypothesis from its operationalisations:

- **UOHF** is one formalisation of the hypothesis, not evidence that the hypothesis is correct.
- The **18 core + 104 subordinate capacity catalogue** is a versioned operationalisation of the capacity domain, not the hypothesis itself.
- **HFWM** is the longitudinal, multiscale extension of the root hypothesis, not a second independent hypothesis and not proof of the first.
- The hypothesis is intended to be testable and potentially refutable through reproducible mapping, comparative interpretation, and prospective explanatory or predictive evaluation.
