## Research question / 核心科学问题

For **the same continuing person**, can explicit, evidence-qualified internal/external demand–capacity–actual-engagement relations improve bounded current-state judgment and time-horizon-specific future prediction over **information-matched, capable alternative models**?

围绕**同一个完整的人**，把内部／外部需求、条件化身体能力、具有独立证据的实际调用、结果、负担、恢复、情境与证据保持区分，是否能在同等信息条件下改善当前状态判断，或改进有明确时间范围的未来预测？

## Proposed research contribution / 拟议研究增量

- **Two temporal clocks / 双时间轴：** Distinguish actual bodily-event time `t` from evidence/knowledge availability cutoff `k`. Late-arriving data may revise an estimate without indicating that the body changed at upload time.
- **Typed present versus future claims / 当前与未来判断分离：** Keep observed fact, estimated current state, conditional observed-policy forecast and causal intervention scenario as separate question types.
- **Demand–capacity–engagement / 需求—能力—调用：** Preserve capacity and independently qualified actual bodily engagement rather than inferring mechanism solely from successful task completion or ICF performance.
- **No look-ahead / 严禁未来信息泄漏：** Predictions use only what was available at their forecast cutoff; planned or hypothetical future demands must not be silently replaced with subsequently realized future conditions.
- **Strong prior-art comparisons / 与已有研究公平比较：** Explicitly compare against Delphi-2M, DT-GPT, Physiological World Models (PWM), Physiome/hybrid models, ICF-informed models and appropriately capable general state engines.
- **Falsifiable evaluation / 可反驳验证：** Compare question-specific warrant, false-change attribution, calibration, longitudinal outcome scores and generalization under matched data, context, relation annotation and compute budgets.

The manuscript defines distinct test tracks: **current-state understanding** and **future state/trajectory prediction**. Good performance on one does not imply validity of the other.

本文分别设立**当前状态理解**和**未来状态与轨迹预测**两条研究路线：其中一条成功不能自动证明另一条成立。

## Evidence maturity / 当前证据边界

**No new independent longitudinal forecasting or clinical validation is reported.** The proposed protocol has not been preregistered or executed as a new prospective study. The manuscript explicitly states that author-run Phase 1–4E engineering tests are narrow checks and **the information-matched general engine obtained the same finite synthetic judgments** in their matched comparison. These checks provide neither demonstrated HFWM predictive superiority nor independently validated capacity/engagement measurement or causal intervention benefit.

**本文没有新增独立纵向预测结果，也没有完成正式预注册或真实临床效果验证。** 原稿明确报告作者自行完成的部分工程检查，在同等规则的有限合成判断中，通用引擎与 HFWM 得到相同结论；这一结果不能被改写为预测优势，也不能充当独立生物学验证。
