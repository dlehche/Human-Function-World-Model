## Focused model-interoperability publication

### Connecting Human Models Through Human Function: Toward a Common Computational Protocol for Whole-Person Modeling

**Author:** Lei Che  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Publication date:** 6 October 2026  
**Status:** Zenodo-archived focused publication  
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23180973](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23180973)  
**License:** CC BY-NC 4.0

- [Overview](human-model-interoperability/README.md)
- [English full text](human-model-interoperability/Human_Model_Interoperability_EN_V1.0.md)
- [中文完整译稿](human-model-interoperability/Human_Model_Interoperability_ZH_V1.0.md)

This paper develops the cross-model interoperability layer: heterogeneous medical, physiological, psychological, cognitive, movement, nutritional, behavioral, environmental, device, and AI models can retain their native meanings while contributing to a shared interpretation of the same continuing person.
