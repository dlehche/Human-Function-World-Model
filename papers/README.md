## Whole-person ontological and computational definition / 完整人本体论与计算科学定义

### What Is a Whole Person? Toward an Ontological and Computational Definition for Whole-Person Modeling

**Author:** Lei Che  
**Manuscript date:** 25 October 2026  
**Status:** Public bilingual repository manuscript; Zenodo DOI reserved, publication not yet confirmed  
**Reserved DOI (not registered):** `10.5281/zenodo.23642975`  
**License:** CC BY-NC 4.0

- [English full text](whole-person-definition/WHAT_IS_A_WHOLE_PERSON_EN.md)
- [中文论文全文](whole-person-definition/WHAT_IS_A_WHOLE_PERSON_ZH.md)
- [Bilingual publication overview and status](whole-person-definition/README.md)

The paper distinguishes the continuing human subject from finite model representations, model scope (P0/P1), and independent task validity (V). It develops an ontological working definition, formal inference constraint, counterexamples, and an evaluation proposal. The two language editions are one study. This paper does not claim a completed validated whole-person simulation or international standard.

## Whole-person comparative conceptual study

### Modeling the Whole Person: Unresolved Questions in Current Medical, Physiological, and Health World Models and a Response from the Human Function World Model

**Author:** Lei Che  
**Publication date:** 11 October 2026  
**Status:** Zenodo-archived bilingual preprint; not peer reviewed  
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23290873](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23290873)  
**License:** CC BY-NC 4.0

- [Publication overview and scientific scope](whole-person-comparative/README.md)
- [Zenodo record — English and Chinese PDFs](https://zenodo.org/records/23290873)

This conceptual comparison evaluates the scope and evidence requirements of existing medical, physiological and health world models, alongside computational digital-twin foundations. It proposes conditional arguments, constructed person-level probes and a falsifiable evaluation programme. It is not an executed computational benchmark or a claim of clinical effectiveness. Both language editions represent one study. It does not replace the canonical HFWM framework publication or the focused interoperability paper.

## Whole-person temporal state understanding and future prediction — focused preprint

### From Human-Function Relations to Whole-Person State Understanding and Future Prediction: A Demand–Capacity–Engagement Temporal Specification, Prior-Art Comparison and Falsifiable Validation Protocol

**Author:** Lei Che ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-8143-1675))  
**Manuscript date:** 4 November 2026 (author-supplied manuscript)  
**Type:** Research methods, prior-art comparison and prospective validation protocol  
**Zenodo DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.23934150](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934150)  
**License:** CC BY-NC 4.0 is the repository default; the Zenodo record license remains to be independently checked  
**Status:** Author-identified Zenodo preprint; no registered prospective study or independent longitudinal forecasting result reported

- [English/Chinese overview and scientific boundaries](papers/whole-person-state-prediction/README.md)
- [Original Zenodo record and deposited publication](https://zenodo.org/records/23934150)
- [Paper-specific citation](papers/whole-person-state-prediction/CITATION.cff)

This paper separates event time from knowledge availability and specifies current-state inference, conditional time-bounded prediction, prior-art comparisons and prospective falsification criteria. It explicitly reports no new forecasting performance or intervention benefit. It is a **different focused study** from the 3 November verifiable-computational-relations methods preprint and does not replace the canonical HFWM V1.0 framework.
