## Research question / 研究问题

What does “whole person” mean as an object of scientific inquiry, and under what declared scope and evidence can a computational system claim to model the same whole person?

“完整人”作为科学研究对象究竟指什么？一个计算系统在什么范围和证据条件下，才能合理声称自己正在建模同一个完整的人？

## Central distinctions / 核心区分

- **The subject / 研究主体：** the real continuing person, not the digital representation.
- **Scope / 模型范围：** bounded specialized models (P0) versus candidate whole-person-oriented architectures (P1).
- **Validation / 验证：** task-specific evidence (V), independent of how broadly a model represents the person.
- **Evidence / 证据：** observations, estimates, predictions, unknowns, and justified conclusions cannot be silently equated.

The paper compares whole-person health, biopsychosocial medicine, digital twins, personal identity, and current whole-person modeling proposals. It presents a working definition, a formal representational-indistinguishability argument, constructive counterexamples, and a proposed independent evaluation strategy. These are conceptual and methodological results, **not completed clinical trials or proof of predictive superiority**.

本文比较全人健康、生物—心理—社会医学、数字孪生、个人身份理论及现有完整个人建模方案，提出工作定义、表示不可区分性的形式论证、构造性反例和独立评价建议；不宣称已完成临床试验或证明预测优势。
