# Research and Collaboration / 研究与合作

HFWM is being developed as a whole-person world-model framework organized through human function.

## Current collaboration priorities

- whole-person world modeling;
- human-function semantics and UOHF;
- internal-task modeling;
- longitudinal state representation and update;
- medicine–human-function bidirectional reasoning;
- physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, audiology, nursing, dietetics, psychology, exercise and training;
- caregiving and environmental coordination;
- multiscale physiological modeling and Whole Person Reference Physiome research;
- Human Reference Atlas and anatomical/spatial knowledge integration;
- Functional Bridge scientific knowledge production and validation;
- evidence-bounded and safety-constrained AI;
- formal verification, competency questions, benchmarks, interoperability and reproducibility.

## Useful forms of contribution

1. conceptual critique and counterexamples;
2. formalization and type-boundary review;
3. professional-scope review;
4. internal-task case studies;
5. longitudinal whole-person case studies;
6. HRA/WPP/VHT interoperability research;
7. benchmark and competency-test design;
8. reproducibility and falsification studies.

## Claim boundaries

Collaboration does not imply completed clinical validation, complete whole-body simulation, autonomous diagnosis, autonomous treatment or access to proprietary production implementation.
